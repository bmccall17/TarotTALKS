# v1.5.4 - Platform Style AI Learning System 🤖

**Released:** Feb 5, 2026

## Summary
Completes Phases 2-4 of the Platform Style Learning System, adding AI-powered analysis of your social media posts to generate platform-specific draft suggestions that match your personal writing style.

---

## Features

### Phase 2: AI-Enhanced Insights
- AI analysis of your posts using Gemini integration
- Detects tone, structure, sentiment patterns per platform
- 7-day caching with content-hash invalidation
- Cost tracking via existing `logApiCall` infrastructure

### Phase 3: Draft Generation
- "Suggest Draft" button in NewShareForm
- Generates platform-specific posts matching your learned style
- Style compliance indicators (length, hashtags, emojis, mentions)
- DraftGenerator modal with copy/use functionality

### Phase 4: Continuous Learning
- Freshness tracking with `last_analyzed_at` and `posts_analyzed`
- `getStalePlatforms()` query function for detecting when re-analysis needed

### Cost Warning Modals
- `CostWarningModal.tsx` component with:
  - Estimated cost display ($0.0005 per AI insight, $0.0003 per draft)
  - Daily and monthly budget progress bars
  - Real-time budget impact calculation
  - Warning when approaching budget limits
- Integrated into both AI Insights generation and Draft generation
